About Me

HEY, I'M JIŘÍ NĚMČÍK

I'm a 20-year-old indie game developer from the Czech Republic. I got into game development simply because I wanted to create my own worlds and make games that are genuinely fun to play.

I work with Unity and Godot. Most of the projects on this site started as school assignments, but instead of just doing the bare minimum to pass, I usually got carried away and turned them into full games.

I like trying out different ideas and genres, from 3D fantasy RPGs and party games to 2D pixel-art platformers. Right now, I'm focused on building new projects and pushing my skills further with each game I finish.
